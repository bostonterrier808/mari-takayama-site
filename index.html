Classical ballet, on stage and in the studio.

Born in Japan and trained in classical ballet from early childhood, Mari Takayama now dances on New York stages, in roles from Kitri in Don Quixote to the Sugar Plum Fairy. In the studio, she teaches children and adults with the same care, one step at a time.
