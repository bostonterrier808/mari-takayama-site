About me

Mari Takayama is a Japanese ballet dancer based in New York City. Trained in classical ballet from early childhood, she performs on stage, teaches, and creates dance projects that connect her Japanese roots with life in New York.

She believes dance belongs to everyone, from the theater to the park.

Works

TeachingLesson

Private and group ballet lessons for children and adults. Each class starts at the barre and builds clean, strong technique step by step, with attention to each dancer's body and goals.

CommunityRadio Taiso

Japanese morning exercise, shared outdoors in New York. Anyone can join, whatever their age or experience. It is a simple way to move together and start the day.
